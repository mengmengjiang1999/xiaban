PROJECT MODIFICATIONS
Retargeted to the MPFB game_engine skeleton, rest-pose correction, skin/mask
baking, mesh reduction, 1K maximum textures, plain teal shirt, depth-safe solid
and cutout materials, -Z forward, foot-floor calibration, six 60 FPS clips.
Roll was adapted to begin/end crouched and uses no horizontal root motion.
P2 previews run/crouch/roll in place; physical action control belongs to P3.
